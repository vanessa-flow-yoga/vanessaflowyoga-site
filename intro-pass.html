Before you book

Things people ask, answered.

Q.01

New to yoga? Will I feel out of place?

Q.01 · Answer

No. The studio's built for everyone, from first-timers through to seasoned practitioners. Tell us at the door if it's your first class and we'll make sure you're set up.

Q.02

Do I need to be flexible?

Q.02 · Answer

No. Flexibility is a by-product of yoga, not a prerequisite. Come as stiff as you like.

Q.03

What do I wear?

Q.03 · Answer

Anything comfy you can move in. Leggings or shorts, a t-shirt or vest. Bare feet in class.

Q.04

Where's the studio? Is there parking?

Q.04 · Answer

The Beauty Boutique, Link 59 Business Park, Unit 5, Clitheroe BB7 1QJ. Limited parking outside, plus plenty of free parking on nearby streets.

Q.05

What's infrared heat? Is it intense?

Q.05 · Answer

Infrared heats you, not the air. Like winter sunshine. Our hot classes are properly intense, but the heat is kinder on your lungs than a conventional sauna. Worth every drop of sweat.

Q.06

Can I pause my pass if I'm away or ill?

Q.06 · Answer

The 21 days run as a block. If you're away for part of it, the on-demand library means you can keep practising from home until you're back in the studio.

Q.07

What happens after 21 days?

Q.07 · Answer

By 21 days, most people are hooked. The £72 unlimited membership keeps it going for less than one drop-in per week. A limited monthly membership, 10-class pack and pay-as-you-go are there too.

Q.08

Will I be charged again automatically?

Q.08 · Answer

No. The £35 is a one-off. We won't roll you onto a membership without you choosing to first.
