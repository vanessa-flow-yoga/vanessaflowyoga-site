About the studio

Yoga without the pressure.

Forget the idea that you need to be flexible, experienced or confident before you begin. Our teachers meet you exactly where you are, helping you build your practice at your own pace.

Built for the yoga-curious, not the yoga-perfect. With hot yoga, gentle classes, Pilates, Barre and more, you'll find a class that fits your body, your goals and your week.

Hot, warm or ambient

Your practice. Your temperature.

Whether you love the challenge of hot yoga or prefer a cooler room, you can choose what works best for you.

Hot

Feel the heat.

A fully heated room for those who enjoy practising in higher temperatures. The heat is part of the experience, whatever class you choose.

Hot Flow · Hot Pilates · Hot Sequence
Warm

A little extra warmth.

Comfortably warmed to create a softer, more comfortable environment for your nervous system. A popular choice for many different class styles.

Most Flow and Strength classes
Ambient

Room temperature.

Practise without added heat. The same welcoming studio, with classes ranging from energising and strengthening to slow and deeply restorative.

Gentle · Yin · Restorative · Nidra
