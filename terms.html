The 21-Day Intro Pass is available to new members only and can only be purchased once. It gives you access to classes for 21 days from your first visit.

Class packs are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Class packs can't usually be shared, but 10-class packs may be transferred to another person under special circumstances, at our discretion.

6. Health and safety

Yoga is a physical activity, and you take part at your own risk.

Please let your teacher know before class if you have any injuries, health conditions or if you're pregnant so we can adapt the practice where possible.

Always practise within your own limits and stop if something doesn't feel right. If you're unsure whether a class is suitable for you, we recommend speaking to your GP or another qualified healthcare professional before attending.

Our teachers are not medical professionals, and nothing taught during classes should be considered medical advice.

The studio opens 15 minutes before each class. Please arrive within that window and not before, as we may not be able to let you in any earlier. For safety and to avoid disrupting others, we may also not be able to admit late arrivals.

We ask everyone to treat the studio, our teachers and fellow members with kindness and respect. If someone's behaviour affects the experience of others, we reserve the right to ask them to leave.

Please keep your belongings with you. We can't accept responsibility for lost, stolen or damaged personal items.
