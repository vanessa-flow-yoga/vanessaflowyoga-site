## What is aerial yoga?

First things first, let's explain what we're actually talking about.

Aerial yoga has been around for a while now and become super popular in recent years.

It's very different to a more traditional yoga practice you may have experienced on the mat, with the small matter of doing it all in mid-air.

_So what does that mean?_

Basically, you'll be doing various postures, some you're familiar with & some you're not so familiar with both on and off the ground using an aerial hammock suspended from the ceiling.

The hammock supports your body weight, giving you the freedom to glide into a posture and even try out more challenging postures that you might not attempt on the mat.

It also eases the pressure on your knees and it's damn good fun.

Not to mention a fabulous way to build your confidence and boost your mental health - we challenge you not to leave the studio with a huge smile on your face.

## What makes it suitable for beginners?

As we mentioned above, we use the hammock both on the mat as a prop to support our balance and being suspended in the air actually makes it a lot easier to get into certain yoga poses.

Plus, as you're supported by the hammock, it's like you have someone holding you & being there to stop you from falling whilst essentially encouraging you to move deeper into a shape.

Like most types of yoga, there are modifications available for every posture to accommodate beginners and more seasoned yogis, so do not fear if you are unable to do something our fabulous teacher will always be able to help you.

## What are the benefits of aerial yoga?

You don't just look like a graceful acrobat as you glide through your aerial yoga postures - there are actually lots of mind and body benefits to this form of exercise.

Advantages include:

- Builds core **strength and stability** by engaging multiple muscle groups at the same time
- Improves **balance and coordination** through dynamic movements
- Helps to decompress the spine, easing **back pain**
- Increases blood circulation and lymphatic flow, promoting **overall health and wellbeing**
- Encourages **mindfulness and mental clarity**
- It's a childlike kind of **fun** - keeping your practice fresh and exciting
- Allows you to achieve deeper stretches, boosting your **flexibility and range of motion**
- Improves **joint mobility and reduces stiffness** so you feel more comfortable and capable
- Supports better **body awareness**
- Adaptable for **all fitness levels** - making it a great low impact exercise for beginners and a fun challenge for seasoned aerial yoga enthusiasts.
