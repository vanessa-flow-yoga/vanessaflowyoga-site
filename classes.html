The best hot yoga in Ribble Valley

Infrared heated yoga, in the Ribble Valley.

We are one of the only studios in the area with infrared heat. It warms your muscles from the inside out, so you move more freely, settle into postures sooner, and go a little deeper without forcing anything. Steady, comfortable warmth, not a wall of heat at the door.

Prefer to keep it cool? Plenty of our timetable is unheated, so you can pick the temperature that suits you on the day.

New to the heat? Bring a towel and a bottle of water, come with a light stomach, and work at your own pace. Beginners are welcome in every heated class.

Pregnancy yoga

Yoga that moves with your pregnancy.

Classes run from 14 weeks to full term, built around the pregnant body. Gentle movement, breath work and relaxation to help you feel strong, calm and ready for birth.

Ease the aches, build strength for labour, learn active birthing positions, and pick up breathing techniques that genuinely help on the day. You will meet other mums-to-be along the way, so it is good for your body and your week.

Sunday evenings, weekly. Suitable from your second trimester onwards. If anything about your pregnancy needs extra care, have a quick word with your midwife or GP first.
