The basics

Things people ask, answered.

Q.01

I have never done yoga. Is that ok?

Q.01 · Answer

More than ok, it is who we are built for. Every class welcomes complete beginners, and the teachers guide you through every step.

Q.02

Do I need to be fit or flexible?

Q.02 · Answer

No. You get more flexible by practising, not the other way round. You start exactly where you are.

Q.03

What should I bring?

Q.03 · Answer

Just yourself and some water. Mats are available to rent, and comfy clothes you can move in are all you need.

Q.04

How hot is the heated studio?

Q.04 · Answer

Warm and comfortable, like a hug for your joints. We use infrared heat, and plenty of classes are unheated if you prefer.

Q.05

What is the 21-day intro pass?

Q.05 · Answer

Twenty-one days of unlimited classes for a flat £35. Every style, no auto-renewal, and you can bring a friend for free.

Q.06

Can men come too?

Q.06 · Answer

All genders, all ages, all body shapes. Everyone is welcome.

Q.07

How do I book?

Q.07 · Answer

Everything is booked online through our timetable. Pick a class, reserve your spot, and turn up a few minutes early.

Q.08

What if I need to cancel a class?

Q.08 · Answer

No problem. Cancel through the Momence app or booking system and give us a bit of notice. Read the cancellation policy for the notice periods and what happens if you cancel late.

Q.09

Where are you and is there parking?

Q.09 · Answer

We are at The Beauty Boutique, Link 59 Business Park, Unit 5, Clitheroe BB7 1QJ, with parking on site.

Q.10

What can I expect on my first visit?

Q.10 · Answer

A big smile and a friendly welcome. You'll soon discover we're a kind, varied and genuinely "normal" bunch, all here to find a little freedom and feel happier. Come 15 minutes early, introduce yourself to your teacher at reception, and we'll get you checked in and settled before class.

Q.11

Can I buy a one-off class?

Q.11 · Answer

Yes. You can drop in for a single class through Momence, or save with a class pack or membership if you'd like to come regularly.
