Our story

Yoga should feel like somewhere you belong.

Yoga isn't about being the most flexible person in the room or trying to keep up with everyone else. It's about showing up exactly as you are, whether it's your first class or your five hundredth.

Before opening Vanessa Flow, Vanessa spent ten years in the dance industry. After years of pressure to perform and look perfect, discovering yoga was a breath of fresh air. It was challenging, rewarding and, most importantly, free from comparison.

That experience shaped everything the studio is today. A welcoming space where people feel comfortable from their very first class, led by a team of teachers who all share the same belief: yoga should be kind, approachable and for everybody.

Vanessa · Founder
The studio

A space designed to help you switch off.

Our welcoming studio sits above The Beauty Boutique in the heart of the Ribble Valley. It's home to hot yoga, Pilates, Barre, gentle yoga, restorative classes and more, all in one calming space.

The room is heated differently depending on the class, from infrared-heated hot yoga to warm and unheated sessions, giving you the freedom to choose what feels right for you.

Forgot your mat? You can hire one at the studio. Just bring yourself.

Pilates

I grew up in the Ribble Valley and have a background in dance, where I discovered Pilates and experienced its benefits both physically and mentally. That journey shapes the way I teach today, with a strong focus on control, quality of movement and attention to detail. My classes focus on building core strength and connecting breath with movement.
