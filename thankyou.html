Good to know

Before your class.

A few simple things to help your first session go smoothly.

01

Arrive early

Come 5 to 10 minutes before the start so you can settle in and set up your space.

02

Bring water

Bring a bottle of water, and a towel too if you are booked into a heated class.

03

Wear comfy kit

Anything you can move in. Mats are here to rent, so there is no need to lug your own.

04

Let us know

Come with a light stomach, and tell your teacher about any injuries or if you are pregnant.
